About Kisan_Setu AI

Our Mission

To empower small and marginal farmers in Bihar with affordable, accessible AI-driven agricultural guidance. We believe that technology should be simple, local, and designed for everyone—regardless of digital literacy.

Our Vision

A future where every farmer has access to trusted crop, weather, and market advice through a single intelligent assistant that understands their language and local conditions. We envision increased farm productivity, better income, and sustainable agriculture across Bihar.
