Pest & Disease Guide

Identify and manage common pests and diseases in Bihari crops

🐜 Aphids

Symptoms:
Yellowing leaves, curling, sticky residue, whitish powder on leaves. Stunted growth.
Affected Crops:
Wheat, Mustard, Pulses, Vegetables
Management:
Spray neem oil spray (3%) or insecticide. Use reflective mulches. Remove affected leaves early.

🍂 Leaf Rust

Symptoms:
Brown/orange pustules on leaf undersides, yellowing around spots, premature leaf drop.
Affected Crops:
Wheat, Barley, Oats
Management:
Use resistant varieties. Spray sulphur or fungicides like Mancozeb when needed. Improve spacing for airflow.

🦟 Stem Borer

Symptoms:
Holes in stems, weak tillers, broken stems, "dead heart" appearance in young plants.
Affected Crops:
Rice, Maize, Sugarcane, Wheat
Management:
Remove affected tillers, use pheromone traps, spray Quinalphos 1.5% or Bacillus thuringiensis.

🐛 Pod Borer

Symptoms:
Holes in pods, damaged seeds, frass (droppings) inside pods, immature seed fall.
Affected Crops:
Chickpea, Pigeonpea, Mustard, Tomato
Management:
Bacillus thuringiensis spray, hand-pick affected pods, use neem oil, apply carbaryl 50 WP.

☁️ Powdery Mildew

Symptoms:
White powdery coating on leaves, stems, flowers. Leaves become pale and distorted.
Affected Crops:
Mustard, Coriander, Grapes, Vegetables
Management:
Spray sulphur 0.5% or Trichoderma powder. Reduce humidity, improve spacing, use resistant varieties.

💥 Blast Disease

Symptoms:
Gray-green spots on leaves/panicles, broke necks in advanced stage, rotted grain.
Affected Crops:
Rice (mainly), Wheat, Barley
Management:
Reduce nitrogen fertilizer, use resistant varieties, spray Tricyclazole or Propiconazole at flag leaf stage.

💡 General Pest Management Tips

  1. Scout regularly: Check fields in early morning for pest presence.
  2. Use resistant varieties: Choose pest-resistant seeds for your region.
  3. Maintain field hygiene: Remove weeds and crop residues.
  4. Practice crop rotation: Change crops annually to break pest cycles.
  5. Use balanced nutrition: Healthy plants resist pests better.
  6. Encourage natural enemies: Keep flowering plants to attract beneficial insects.
  7. Use pesticides wisely: Follow dosage, coat entire plant, and observe harvest interval.
  8. Keep records: Document pest appearance for future reference.
